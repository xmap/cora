# Fleet recurrence: <FACILITY>

Cross-fleet device-class frequency across the beamlines surveyed under `research/<facility>/beamlines/`. The point of this report is the **catalog Family graduation signal**: a device class that recurs across two or more *physically distinct* beamlines is a graduation candidate (rule-of-three is the firm trigger; two is a watch). `graduated` marks classes already in `catalog/catalog.yaml`.

For an EPICS `*-bits` facility this file is generated by `scripts/reverse_engineer` (`--recurrence-out`). For a hand-surveyed facility (BLISS / Tango / firewalled), fill it by hand from the per-beamline `facts.md` "new-family watch" sections.

!!! warning "Count physical beamlines, not repos"
    A fork or a multi-endstation repo inflates the count. Deduplicate to physical beamlines before treating a count as a trigger: two endstations of one beamline, or a fork of another beamline's repo, are one data point, not two. Note any such collapse inline.
