-   __Scrubbing the Run: Replay-Native Provenance Visualization for Agent-Supervised Beamline Experiments__

    *VAxAutoSci · IEEE VIS 2026 · Boston · Doğa Gürsoy, Francesco De Carlo*

    **Drafting, target July 2026.** When the system of record is event-sourced, provenance becomes something you can scrub. The backend fold that rebuilds state turns into a draggable time cursor over a recorded autonomous run, paired with a lane showing who or what drove each step and a fidelity badge proving the replay is faithful. We show it on a real unattended run on the APS 2-BM micro-CT beamline, including a beam loss that a supervisor agent holds and auto-resumes.
