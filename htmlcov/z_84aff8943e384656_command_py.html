1"""The `RemoveModelFamily` command, intent dataclass for this slice. 

2 

3Targeted-mutation: incremental removal of a single Family from the 

4Model's `declared_family_ids` set. Sibling of `add_model_family`. 

5 

6The operational pattern is "vendor firmware update drops support for 

7a technique" or "catalog entry no longer advertises a Family"; 

8`version_model` remains the path when a revision genuinely re-authors 

9the catalog entry (matches the Family/Method/Plan/Practice 

10replace-on-version precedent). 

11 

12`model_id` is the target Model aggregate. `family_id` is the Family 

13being undeclared. Unlike `add_model_family`, the handler does NOT 

14resolve the Family against the Family registry: removal only 

15requires that `family_id` already sits in `declared_family_ids` (the 

16Family may have been deprecated or deleted, and removal still 

17proceeds). 

18 

19Strict-not-idempotent: removing a Family not in `declared_family_ids` 

20raises `ModelFamilyNotPresentError` (same precedent as 

21`add_model_family` and `remove_asset_family`). 

22"""

25from uuid import UUID
