1"""Role aggregate state, errors, and value objects. 

2 

3`Role` is the Equipment BC's global functional binding contract: WHAT 

4operational shape a Method needs (`Detector`, `Positioner`, `Controller`, 

5`Sensor`) without pinning the anatomical Family that provides it. 

6Per [[project-role-aggregate-design]] Lock 1, Role is a sister 

7aggregate to Family, NOT a kind of Family: separate registry, separate 

8events, separate identifier (`RoleId`). 

9 

10## Aggregate scope (Layer 3 sub-slice 3A) 

11 

12Lightweight contract only (Lock 2): `required_affordances` + 

13`optional_affordances` + `produces` + `consumes` + `docstring`. No 

14`settings_schema`, no instances, no ports. Role is templated, not 

15instantiated: a Family or Assembly declares it satisfies a Role via 

16`presents_as: frozenset[RoleId]` (3B / 3C), and a Method binds a 

17positional role slot to a Role via `RoleRequirement.role_kind: UUID` 

18(3D). The Plan-side satisfaction check at `bind_plan_role` walks 

19`Family.presents_as` and requires the Family's `affordances` superset 

20the Role's `required_affordances` (ANY-single-family 

21disjunction for multi-Family Assets). 

22 

23## No FSM at 3A (Q1 user pick, 2026-06-10) 

24 

253A ships ONE event (`RoleDefined`); update events 

26(`RoleAffordancesUpdated`, `RoleSignalsUpdated`) are deferred until 

27the Lock 14 SiLA-2 FQN-terminal-major versioning trigger fires (first 

28`required_affordances` change that breaks an existing Asset's 

29satisfaction). Shipping update events at 3A would lock the SHAPE of 

30versioning before the trigger fires, which is exactly what Lock 14 

31defers. No `RoleStatus` enum at this slice; status is implicit 

32(`Defined`) on every Role until versioning lands. 

33 

34## Bounded-name VO 

35 

36`RoleName` follows the trimmed-bounded-name VO pattern; uses the 

37shared `validate_bounded_text` helper. NewType `RoleId` lives at 

38`cora/equipment/aggregates/_value_types.py` (CredentialId precedent 

39from Slice 6; keeps the fold-symmetry test's attribution-NewType 

40allowlist clean). 

41 

42## RoleName vs RoleKindName terminology note 

43 

44The Method-local positional label (slice 1, shipped) is the per-Method 

45operator handle, e.g. `DETECTOR`, `SAMPLE_MONITOR`. The global Role 

46contract's display name (this aggregate) is also called `RoleName` in 

47the codebase; the design memo uses `RoleKindName` to disambiguate in 

48prose. The two are distinct concepts at distinct layers (Method-local 

49vs global registry) but the slice-1 `RoleName` type already lives in 

50the Recipe BC under `cora.recipe.aggregates.method`, so the global 

51contract's name is wrapped here as `RoleName` without name collision 

52(no cross-BC import). Cross-BC consumers grep on the qualified import 

53path to disambiguate. 

54"""

105class RoleAffordanceOverlapError(ValueError): 

106 """`required_affordances` and `optional_affordances` are not disjoint. 

107 

108 A Role's required and optional Affordance sets MUST be disjoint: 

109 "optional" is meaningful only as a strictly-stronger contract than 

110 "required absent", and listing the same Affordance in both sets 

111 signals operator confusion. Caught at decider time. 

112 """ 

113 

114 def __init__(self, role_id: UUID, overlap: frozenset[Affordance]) -> None: 

115 super().__init__( 

116 f"Role {role_id} has Affordances appearing in both required and " 

117 f"optional sets: {sorted(a.value for a in overlap)!r}" 

118 ) 

119 self.role_id = role_id 

120 self.overlap = overlap

131@dataclass(frozen=True) 

132class Role: 

133 """Aggregate root: a global functional binding contract. 

134 

135 `required_affordances` lists Affordances every satisfying Family 

136 MUST advertise. `optional_affordances` lists Affordances a Family 

137 MAY advertise to satisfy a stronger variant of the same Role 

138 (Method consumers gate on optional independently). The two sets 

139 are disjoint (`RoleAffordanceOverlapError` at decider time). 

140 

141 `produces` and `consumes` declare the SignalType vocabulary 

142 satisfying Assets emit (out ports) and accept (in ports). Used by 

143 Plan-side wiring guidance (informative at 3A; the bind_plan_role 

144 satisfaction check in 3D gates on `presents_as` + affordances 

145 superset, not on signal types). 

146 

147 `docstring` is the operator-readable one-paragraph contract 

148 explanation. Required (non-empty) so operators picking among Roles 

149 at Method-authoring time see the contract intent without spelunking 

150 through code. 

151 """ 

152
