1"""Slice-local cross-aggregate context for `bind_plan_role`. 

2 

3Threads the Plan's bound Method, the candidate Asset, and the 

4Layer 3 sub-slice 3D RoleLookup + FamilyLookup + AssemblyLookup 

5results into the pure decider so the decider stays I/O-free. 

6 

7`method` MAY be None when the Plan's `state.method_id` is None: 

8that would be a legacy Plan whose define-time event lacked the 

9field, and the decider treats this as `MethodNotFoundError`. 

10 

11`role_lookup_result` is populated by the handler ONLY when the 

12matching RoleRequirement (looked up by role_name) carries 

13`role_kind` (the 3D path). None when the matching RoleRequirement 

14is family_id-only (the slice-1 anatomical escape hatch). Also 

15None when the role_name is not declared on the Method (decider 

16surfaces PlanRoleNameNotDeclaredError before consulting this). 

17 

18`family_lookups` is a dict keyed by family_id mapping to the 

19FamilyLookupResult the handler loaded for each family in 

20`asset.family_ids`. Populated alongside `role_lookup_result` for 

21the 3D role_kind path; empty when the slice-1 family_id path is 

22in play (decider does not consult). 

23 

24`assembly_lookup_result` is populated by the handler ONLY when 

25(a) the matching RoleRequirement carries `role_kind` AND (b) the 

26candidate Asset carries `fixture_id` (the Asset is part of a 

27materialized Assembly). The decider ORs-in 

28`role_kind in assembly.presents_as` on top of the Family 

29disjunction so a composed Assembly (e.g. MCTOptics) can satisfy 

30the Role even when no individual Family in `asset.family_ids` 

31declares it. None when the Asset is not in a Fixture or when 

32the Fixture / Assembly projection lookup misses (the Family path 

33may still succeed; if it does not the existing 

34PlanRoleAssetCannotPresentError fires). 

35""" 

36
