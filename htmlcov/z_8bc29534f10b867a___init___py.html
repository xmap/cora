1"""Record export: CORA's record as a hashed, offline-verifiable artifact. 

2 

3The design is `project_record_export_v3.md`, locked for the no-beam 

4commissioning scope. This package holds the exporter and the generated 

5redaction disposition table it reads. 

6 

7It lives at `cora.infrastructure`, whose `tach.toml` entry allows 

8`cora.shared` and nothing else, and that constraint is the guarantee 

9rather than the obstacle: the exporter composes nothing (column-driven 

10over raw rows, a registry of `str -> str` pairs, and a table read as 

11data), so the layering rule ENFORCES the zero-bounded-context-import 

12property that makes the standalone-verifiability claim honest. If a 

13change here wants to import an aggregate, the design has drifted. 

14 

15Two things this package must never do, both learned from review rather 

16than from first principles: 

17 

18- Drive extraction, redaction or parity from `LogbookSchema`. 

19 `LogbookFieldType` is closed over six scalars, so no schema can name a 

20 jsonb column, and jsonb is where the doing lives. The schema travels 

21 with the export as documentation. 

22- Describe the published record as anonymous. It is pseudonymous: 

23 timestamps plus a facility's own published beamtime schedule 

24 re-identify without the token map. 

25"""
