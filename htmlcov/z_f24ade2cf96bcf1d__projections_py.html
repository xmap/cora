1"""Access BC's projection-registration entry point. 

2 

3The composition root (`cora.api.main`) calls 

4`register_access_projections(registry, deps)` during the FastAPI 

5lifespan to populate the worker's registry with every Access BC 

6projection. New projections are added here as a one-line 

7`registry.register(...)` call. 

8 

9Lives outside `wire.py` to keep handler-wiring and projection-wiring 

10separated (handlers go on app.state.access; projections go on the 

11worker's registry). 

12""" 

13

18

23 """Register every Access-owned projection on the worker registry. 

24 

25 `deps` is accepted (and currently unused) so projections that need

28 subscribed_event_types so `deps` stays unused.
