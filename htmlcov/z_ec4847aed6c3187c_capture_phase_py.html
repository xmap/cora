1"""CapturePhase: the facility-neutral lifecycle phase of an observed capture. 

2 

3Originated on the Run BC's `CaptureObserver` port and hoisted here for 

4the same reason `ReachTier` was: `cora.infrastructure` (where 

5`Settings.capture_status_phases` validates a deployment's declared 

6literal-to-phase mapping) cannot depend on `cora.run.ports` (tach: BCs 

7depend on infrastructure, never the reverse), so the one enum both 

8sides need to agree on has to live below both of them. 

9""" 

10 

11from enum import StrEnum 

12 

13 

14class CapturePhase(StrEnum): 

15 """The facility-neutral lifecycle phase of an observed capture. 

16 

17 Closed and small on purpose: this is the vocabulary CORA's spine 

18 reasons over, not the vocabulary any one facility's tool emits. 

19 `UNRECOGNIZED` is a first-class member, not an absence: a substrate 

20 literal that does not match the deployment's declared mapping 

21 reports `UNRECOGNIZED` rather than being coerced into a nearby 

22 phase or dropped silently, so a vocabulary drift (a tool upgrade 

23 that renames a status) is visible in the record rather than 

24 misread as routine progress. 

25 """ 

26 

27 BEGUN = "Begun" 

28 PROGRESSING = "Progressing" 

29 ENDED = "Ended" 

30 ABORTED = "Aborted" 

31 UNRECOGNIZED = "Unrecognized" 

32 

33 

34__all__ = ["CapturePhase"]
