1"""Edge-auth plumbing package. 

2 

3Holds the auth-edge pieces that are NOT `TokenVerifier` port adapters: 

4 

5 - `BearerAuthMiddleware` (Starlette middleware) — verifies inbound 

6 `Authorization: Bearer` tokens via the kernel's configured verifier. 

7 - `IdentityProviderRegistry` — process-singleton router that picks the 

8 right `TokenVerifier` adapter per token's issuer. 

9 - `IdentityProviderConfig` + `IdpSubjectBinding` + `StaticSubjectMapper` 

10 — config + subject-mapping helpers. 

11 - `build_idp_registry`, `build_static_subject_mapper` — composition-root 

12 factories. 

13 - `exception_handlers.py` — FastAPI exception handler functions that 

14 convert auth errors into RFC 6750 401 / RFC 7231 503 responses. 

15 

16The `TokenVerifier` port adapters themselves live at 

17`cora.infrastructure.adapters.jwt_token_verifier::JwtTokenVerifier` and 

18`cora.infrastructure.adapters.introspection_token_verifier::IntrospectionTokenVerifier` 

19per the locked `<Tech><Port>` naming rule. 

20 

21Per the edge-auth design lock library-vs-DIY decision: PyJWT is the 

22one library dependency; everything else is hand-written. 

23""" 

24
