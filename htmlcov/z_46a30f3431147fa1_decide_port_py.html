1"""DecidePort: the domain-shaped seam over which CORA asks an external brain 

2"given everything measured so far, what should I measure next, or stop?". 

3 

4`DecidePort` is the DECIDE-axis sibling of `ControlPort` and `ComputePort`. 

5ControlPort drives hardware value-IO; ComputePort drives a compute job; 

6DecidePort drives the CHOICE of the next acquisition. It lives in the same 

7BC home (`cora.operation.ports`) as its siblings, and reuses their value 

8types (`Measurement`, `ArtifactRef`, `ActuationKind`) field-for-field, so a 

9conduct result maps onto a steering observation with no translation layer. 

10It is homed here, not in the Decision BC, because tach forbids 

11`cora.decision` from importing `cora.operation.ports` (where those value 

12types live) and the first caller is the Conductor (Operation BC). The 

13Decision BC keeps owning the `Decision` aggregate; an across-Run steerer 

14(deferred) records its rationale there via the signed-subscriber path. 

15 

16## Optimizer- and action-neutral by construction 

17 

18The brain behind the seam may be a static grid walker, a Bayesian 

19optimizer, or an LLM agent; CORA cannot tell which, and must not. So: 

20 

21 - Optimizer internals (kernel, acquisition function, surrogate model, GP 

22 posterior, exploration weight) NEVER cross the seam: they live inside 

23 the adapter and its config. 

24 - Control specifics (PV / motor / trigger / scan / acquire / the 

25 conductor's captures bus) NEVER cross the seam: a `next_point` is a set 

26 of coordinates keyed by axis NAME, not a command. Translating a point 

27 into Conductor steps is the caller's job, not the port's. 

28 

29`SteeringObjective` names WHAT good means by a Measurement NAME, origin- 

30agnostic (a control read OR a compute output), which is the single decision 

31that keeps a compute-steering brain expressible with these same DTOs. 

32 

33## The six-noun kernel 

34 

35Every autonomous experiment, on any beamline, with any brain, needs the 

36same six things; remove one and the loop is undecidable: 

37 

38 1. `SteeringObjective` - what good means. 

39 2. `SteeringSpace` / `SteeringAxis` - where we may look. 

40 3. `SteeringEvidence` - what has happened (the full history, handed to a 

41 STATELESS brain; CORA never assumes the brain remembers prior calls). 

42 4. `SteeringObservation` - one datum of that history. 

43 5. `SteeringAdvice` - what to do next, and whether to quit. 

44 6. `SteeringBudget` - how much is left. 

45 

46`SteeringPoint` is the derived coordinate value type the brain proposes and 

47the caller translates. 

48 

49## Stateless brain 

50 

51`advise_next` hands over the full `SteeringEvidence.observations` every 

52call, so a brain that holds no memory (an LLM, a pure function) and a brain 

53that caches a surrogate internally (a GP) both satisfy the same surface. 

54The port promises nothing about cross-call memory, so it never carries a 

55session handle; a stateful adapter rebuilds from the full history. 

56 

57## Exceptions 

58 

59Five exception families mirror CORA's standard shape and the ControlPort / 

60ComputePort posture: `DecidePort` is not REST-accessible. The caller folds 

61a raised exception into a recorded steering decision (deferred / rejected) 

62rather than crashing the loop, exactly as the executor folds ControlPort 

63exceptions into event-payload metadata per 

64[[project_non_determinism_principle]]. 

65 

66## Earned, not minted 

67 

68Ships with one test fake (`InMemoryDecidePort`) plus, in the next slice, a 

69single in-CORA `GridWalkDecidePort` (no external optimizer). A routing 

70registry and a real GP adapter (gpCAM) are earned at their own triggers, 

71exactly as ControlPort earned its registry from a third substrate and 

72ComputePort deferred its registry to a second. 

73"""

100class SteeringVerdict(StrEnum): 

101 """The brain's continue-or-quit decision, fused with its suggestion. 

102 

103 `Measure`: take another acquisition at the advised `next_point`. 

104 `Stop`: the brain is done (objective met, space exhausted, or it judges 

105 further measurement not worthwhile). A `Stop` carries no `next_point`. 

106 

107 The verdict is fused into `SteeringAdvice` (not a separate port call) 

108 because a brain decides 'continue vs stop' and 'where' in one inference; 

109 splitting them would let the two answers disagree. 

110 """ 

111 

112 MEASURE = "Measure" 

113 STOP = "Stop"

117class SteeringObservation: 

118 """One prior trial: we measured AT `point` and got THIS. 

119 

120 Reuses `Measurement` / `ArtifactRef` / `ActuationKind` field-for-field 

121 from the conduct result so the caller maps a `ConductorResult` onto an 

122 observation with no translation. `succeeded=False` with empty 

123 `measurements` is legal and first-class: a failed acquisition is a data 

124 point (a region to avoid), not something the caller silently drops.

126 point; the adapter may also ignore it. 

127 """

130 measurements: tuple[Measurement, ...] = ()

132 actuation_kind: ActuationKind | None = None 

133 succeeded: bool = True

152@dataclass(frozen=True) 

153class SteeringEvidence: 

154 """The full picture handed to a stateless brain on each `advise_next`. 

155 

156 `objective` + `space` say what good means and where to look; the ordered 

157 `observations` are the history so the brain reconstructs context every 

158 call; `budget` + `iteration_index` let it (and the caller's guard) reason 

159 about exhaustion. The scope ids are OPTIONAL and additive: the 

160 in-conductor home fills `procedure_id`; an across-Run steerer (deferred) 

161 fills `campaign_id` + `run_id`. One value type, two homes. 

162 

163 `iteration_index` follows the first-class iteration naming 

164 (`iteration_index` / `iteration_count`); it is the 0-based loop turn the 

165 caller is on. 

166 """ 

167 

168 objective: SteeringObjective 

169 space: SteeringSpace 

170 observations: tuple[SteeringObservation, ...] = () 

171 budget: SteeringBudget = field(default_factory=SteeringBudget) 

172 iteration_index: int = 0 

173 procedure_id: UUID | None = None 

174 run_id: UUID | None = None 

175 campaign_id: UUID | None = None

178class DecideNotAvailableError(Exception): 

179 """The decider could not be reached at all. 

180 

181 Triggered when an optimizer service / subprocess / model endpoint is 

182 unreachable or unconfigured. A configuration / environment gap, not a 

183 per-call rejection. The caller folds this into a deferred steering 

184 decision rather than crashing the loop. 

185 """ 

186 

187 def __init__(self, reason: str) -> None: 

188 super().__init__(f"Decider not available: {reason}") 

189 self.reason = reason

219class DecideColdStartError(DecideEvidenceRejectedError): 

220 """The decider refused because the history is too small YET, not wrong. 

221 

222 A TRANSIENT subtype of `DecideEvidenceRejectedError`: the brain is 

223 reachable and the request is well-formed, but there are too few usable 

224 observations to act on (a GP needs seed points before a fit is 

225 meaningful). More evidence fixes it. Distinct from a permanent 

226 `DecideEvidenceRejectedError` (an unsupported objective kind, a missing 

227 target, a non-continuous axis), which more evidence never fixes. 

228 

229 The distinction is load-bearing for `StagedDecidePort`: it catches THIS 

230 subtype to fall back to its seeder for another point, keeping the loop 

231 accreting usable observations, while it lets a permanent rejection 

232 propagate so a misconfigured objective ends the run instead of seeding 

233 forever. Being a subclass, it is still folded by the conduct loop's 

234 `Decide*Error` handling and still matched by callers catching the base 

235 `DecideEvidenceRejectedError`. 

236 """

269@dataclass(frozen=True) 

270class SteeringAdvice: 

271 """The decider's only output: a next action, or a stop, with provenance. 

272 

273 `verdict` fuses continue-vs-stop with the suggestion: `Measure` carries 

274 a `next_point`, `Stop` carries none. `rationale` / `confidence` / 

275 `confidence_source` / `alternatives` / `model_ref` are the provenance a 

276 caller records (the in-conductor home onto the iteration ledger; an 

277 across-Run steerer onto a `Decision`). `confidence_source` reuses the 

278 shared `DecisionConfidenceSource` so a recorded confidence carries the 

279 same ISO-42001 derivation label whatever the home. 

280 

281 Self-validating at construction (raising `DecideAdviceMalformedError`) 

282 so a malformed brain answer cannot enter the loop: confidence stays in 

283 [0.0, 1.0] (NaN rejected), the rationale fits `REASONING_MAX_LENGTH`, 

284 and the verdict / `next_point` pairing is consistent. 

285 """ 

286 

287 verdict: SteeringVerdict 

288 next_point: SteeringPoint | None = None 

289 rationale: str | None = None 

290 confidence: float | None = None 

291 confidence_source: DecisionConfidenceSource | None = None 

292 alternatives: tuple[str, ...] = () 

293 model_ref: str | None = None 

294 

295 def __post_init__(self) -> None: 

296 if self.verdict is SteeringVerdict.MEASURE and self.next_point is None: 

297 raise DecideAdviceMalformedError("Measure verdict requires a next_point") 

298 if self.verdict is SteeringVerdict.STOP and self.next_point is not None: 

299 raise DecideAdviceMalformedError("Stop verdict must not carry a next_point") 

300 if self.confidence is not None: 

301 if self.confidence != self.confidence: # NaN check (NaN != NaN) 

302 raise DecideAdviceMalformedError("confidence is NaN") 

303 if not (0.0 <= self.confidence <= 1.0): 

304 raise DecideAdviceMalformedError( 

305 f"confidence {self.confidence!r} outside [0.0, 1.0]" 

306 ) 

307 if self.rationale is not None and len(self.rationale) > REASONING_MAX_LENGTH: 

308 raise DecideAdviceMalformedError(f"rationale exceeds {REASONING_MAX_LENGTH} chars")

331 """Map a `SteeringAdvice` onto its decision-provenance fields.

347def objective_is_satisfied( 

348 objective: SteeringObjective, measurements: tuple[Measurement, ...] 

349) -> bool: 

350 """True when a `Satisfy` objective's target_value is met exactly by `measurements`. 

351 

352 The single definition of "the objective is met", shared by every consumer that 

353 needs it: the within-loop brain (`GridWalkDecidePort`'s early Stop, passing its 

354 latest observation's measurements) and the across-procedure driver (the 

355 ExperimentSteerer's Continue-vs-Conclude rule, passing the conduct result's 

356 measurements). Takes the measurement tuple directly, not a whole 

357 `SteeringObservation`, so a caller that holds only a `ConductorResult` (no 

358 point) does not fabricate one. Non-`Satisfy` objectives (Minimize / Maximize / 

359 Explore) have no exact-met notion here and return False (a smarter brain owns 

360 their stop). A tolerance band is a deferred widening; v1 is exact equality on 

361 the named measurement. 

362 """ 

363 if objective.kind is not SteeringObjectiveKind.SATISFY: 

364 return False 

365 if objective.target_value is None or objective.target_measurement_name is None: 

366 return False 

367 return any( 

368 m.name == objective.target_measurement_name and m.value == objective.target_value 

369 for m in measurements 

370 )

373@runtime_checkable 

374class DecidePort(Protocol): 

375 """Domain-shaped decide seam for autonomous experimentation. 

376 

377 Optimizer- and action-agnostic. Concrete deciders (`InMemoryDecidePort`, 

378 a future `GridWalkDecidePort`, a future GP / LLM adapter) implement the 

379 brain behind the seam. Per [[project_non_determinism_principle]] the 

380 caller captures the advice onto its event stream at decide time, so a 

381 replay never re-asks the brain. 

382 

383 The port is one advisory STEP, never the loop: the caller owns iterate / 

384 feed-back / stop-guard / conduct. A port that owned the loop could not 

385 host a stateless brain and would duplicate the Conductor's job. 

386 """ 

387 

388 async def advise_next(self, evidence: SteeringEvidence) -> SteeringAdvice: 

389 """Given everything measured so far, advise the next action or stop. 

390 

391 One async request/response per loop iteration: the caller hands over 

392 the accumulated `evidence` and gets back a captured-once 

393 `SteeringAdvice`. Async because the brain is I/O-bound (a model 

394 call, an optimizer service, a subprocess). Raises 

395 `DecideNotAvailableError`, `DecideTimeoutError`, 

396 `DecideEvidenceRejectedError`, `DecideAdviceMalformedError`, or 

397 `DecideAccessDeniedError`; the caller folds any of these into a 

398 deferred steering decision rather than crashing the loop. 

399 """ 

400 ... 

401 

402 async def aclose(self) -> None: 

403 """Release any decider resources; idempotent. 

404 

405 Provided so composition code can `aclose()` any `DecidePort` without 

406 branching on type (mirrors `ControlPort.aclose` / `ComputePort.aclose`). 

407 The in-memory fake is a no-op; an adapter holding a model client pool 

408 or optimizer subprocess releases it here. 

409 """
