1"""Frozen-dataclass intermediate, input view, and closed enums for PIDINST v1.0. 

2 

3The PIDINST integration carves the asset-to-DataCite path into two 

4modules at the Equipment BC root plus one in `errors.py`: 

5 

6 - `_pidinst_types.py` (this file): the intermediate `PidinstRecord` 

7 tree, the `AssetPidinstView` input view, and the four closed 

8 StrEnums (`SchemaVersion`, `DateType`, `PidinstIdentifierType`, 

9 `PidinstRelationType`). 

10 - `_pidinst_serializer.py`: the `to_pidinst_record(view)` pure 

11 function plus its property helpers. 

12 - `cora.equipment.errors`: the five `PidinstSerializationError` 

13 subclasses (four pre-construction errors plus 

14 `PidinstRecordInvariantError`). Co-located with `UnauthorizedError` 

15 per the BC's domain-error placement fitness; the architecture 

16 test `test_no_domain_errors_outside_aggregate_or_errors_module` 

17 forbids error class definitions inside private slice modules. 

18 

19Splitting keeps the slice-6 mint adapter importable against the 

20intermediate types alone without dragging in serializer logic. 

21 

22The `_types` suffix avoids collision with the `_*_body.py` Pydantic 

23wire-mirror convention used by `_alternate_identifier_body`, 

24`_drawing_body`, `_placement_body`. The intermediate here is a 

25frozen-dataclass tree, not a Pydantic wire model. 

26 

27Forward-complete per L7 of the design memo: every recommended PIDINST 

28v1.0 property is represented on `PidinstRecord` even when slice C 

29cannot populate it. Slices D and E populate fields on the unchanged 

30type; only the serializer function is feature-additive. 

31 

32L27 cross-BC import boundary: this module MAY import from 

33`cora.equipment.aggregates.*` and MUST NOT import from any other BC. 

34Enforced by `test_pidinst_serializer_imports_no_other_bc`. 

35 

36L28 typing rigor: NO `Any` and NO `dict[str, Any]` anywhere in this 

37file. Every dataclass field carries a concrete type. Enforced by 

38`test_pidinst_record_has_no_any_or_dict_str_any_annotations`. 

39"""
