1"""ControlPort: domain-shaped value-IO for the Operation BC executor. 

2 

3`ControlPort` is the async Protocol that Operation BC slice handlers 

4(and the future Procedure executor) use to read, write, and subscribe 

5to control-system values. Substrate details (EPICS CA via aioca, 

6EPICS PVA via p4p, Tango via PyTango, OPC UA via asyncua) live behind 

7concrete adapters; the executor never touches substrate-specific 

8symbols directly. 

9 

10Per [[project_control_port_generalization_research]] this supersedes 

11the earlier `PvDriver` design. The supersession was driven by 

12a three-substrate stress test (EPICS + Tango + OPC UA sanity check) 

13showing that value-IO genuinely generalises across all three when 

14the port owns its vocabulary; RPC and typed events do NOT generalise 

15and stay deferred to future sibling ports (`CommandPort`, `EventPort`) 

16per adapter-first discipline. 

17 

18## Domain vocabulary (substrate-neutral) 

19 

20The shared value types `Measurement`, `MeasurementKind`, and `Quality` 

21live in `cora.operation.ports.measurement` (they serve every edge-runtime 

22port, not just control). A `read` returns a `Measurement`; subscribe 

23yields `Measurement` values. See that module for field-by-field detail. 

24 

25## Address space 

26 

27The caller-facing `ControlPort` stays `address: str`. Callers (the 

28Conductor, `acquisitions`, the beam-availability lookup) receive raw 

29address strings from recipe steps and cannot know a substrate: 

30whether `"2bm:rot:rbv"` is EPICS Channel Access, `"2bm:cam1:image"` 

31is EPICS pvAccess, or `"sys/tg_test/1/double_scalar"` is a Tango 

32attribute is a property of the deployment ROUTE table, never of the 

33string. So the string surface is where callers belong. 

34 

35The second-substrate trigger (Tango) fired the typed-sum promotion, 

36but at the layer where substrate identity actually lives: the 

37registry-to-adapter seam. `ControlPortRegistry` matches a route by 

38string prefix, then parses the string into a `ControlAddress` 

39(`EpicsPvAddress | TangoAttributeAddress | InMemoryAddress`, see 

40`cora.operation.ports.control_address`) per the route's declared 

41substrate, and hands the typed variant to a `SubstrateControlPort` 

42adapter. The substrate adapters consume typed addresses; the change 

43is BC-internal and non-breaking outside the Operation BC. OPC UA 

44stays deferred (no `OpcUaNodeAddress` variant until its adapter lands). 

45 

46## Out of scope (deferred sibling ports) 

47 

48- **RPC** (Tango Commands, OPC UA Methods, EPICS V4 RPC): future 

49 `CommandPort` Protocol. Triggers at first concrete RPC consumer. 

50- **Typed events** (Tango `INTERFACE_CHANGE` / `DATA_READY`, OPC UA 

51 `EventNotificationList`): future `EventPort` Protocol. Triggers at 

52 first typed-event consumer. 

53 

54Per [[project_control_port_generalization_research]] anti-hooks: 

55do NOT widen `ControlPort` with `invoke` (RPC) or 

56`subscribe_events` (typed events). Add separate ports when triggered; 

57keep this one value-IO-only. 

58 

59## Exceptions 

60 

61Seven exception families mirror CORA's standard shape. `ControlPort` is 

62not REST-accessible; the executor's decider captures these as 

63event-payload metadata per [[project_non_determinism_principle]]. 

64Six report what the substrate did; `ControlWritesDisabledError` reports 

65what CORA declined to do, before any substrate was contacted. 

66 

67## Subscribe shape 

68 

69`subscribe` is a plain `def` returning `AsyncIterator[Measurement]` directly 

70(no surrounding coroutine). Caller iterates with 

71`async for reading in port.subscribe(address):`. Connect setup may 

72happen lazily on the first `__anext__`; the adapter still raises 

73`ControlNotConnectedError` through the iterator if the address is 

74never reachable. Adapters may coalesce intermediate values on 

75subscriber lag (p4p Qt keep-last; OPC UA MonitoredItem queue 

76semantics). Mid-stream disconnect raises `ControlNotConnectedError` 

77through the iterator so silent stream pause is impossible. 

78"""

97class ActuationKind(StrEnum): 

98 """How a conducted episode drove its addresses: real hardware, a 

99 simulator, or both. 

100 

101 Derived by the Conductor from the per-route simulated flag the 

102 registry carries, and snapshotted (as a raw string) onto the 

103 producing Dataset so simulator-origin data can never be promoted 

104 to Production. Simulated-ness is a declared property of a route 

105 (ControlPortRoute.is_simulated), not the transport substrate: a 

106 soft IOC speaks real Channel Access yet is still a simulator. 

107 

108 Physical: every address routed to a non-simulated adapter. 

109 Simulated: every address routed to a simulated adapter. 

110 Hybrid: a single conduct drove both. The promotion gate treats 

111 Hybrid exactly as Simulated (any simulator touch disqualifies). 

112 """ 

113 

114 PHYSICAL = "Physical" 

115 SIMULATED = "Simulated" 

116 HYBRID = "Hybrid"

149class ControlWriteRejectedError(Exception): 

150 """Substrate rejected the write. 

151 

152 Triggered on read-only address, invalid value type for the 

153 address's substrate type, or substrate-level access-security 

154 denial expressed as a write-callback failure. Distinct from 

155 `ControlAccessDeniedError` so adapters preserve the substrate's 

156 failure-mode distinction (e.g., EPICS IOC put-callback failure 

157 vs Channel Access security denial). 

158 

159 Note the two-tier read-only overlap: the "read-only address" case 

160 here is a read-only RECORD on the IOC, discovered by attempting the 

161 write. `ControlWritesDisabledError` is the CORA-side declaration 

162 that refused before any write was attempted. The remedies are 

163 opposite, so keep them apart when reading logs: this one means the 

164 IOC or its access rules need changing, that one means CORA's 

165 configuration does. 

166 """ 

167 

168 def __init__(self, address: str, reason: str) -> None: 

169 super().__init__(f"Control address {address!r} write rejected: {reason}") 

170 self.address = address 

171 self.reason = reason

174class ControlWritesDisabledError(Exception): 

175 """CORA declares this address unwritable; no write was attempted. 

176 

177 Raised by `ReadOnlyControlPort` when a deployment has declared its 

178 control writes off, either deployment-wide 

179 (`CONTROL_WRITES_ENABLED=false`, `scope="deployment"`) or for the 

180 one route carrying `ControlPortRoute.read_only` (`scope="route"`). 

181 A declared deployment fact, never inferred from the substrate, and 

182 settled before any IO: nothing reached the IOC. 

183 

184 The grammatical subject is what separates this from its siblings. 

185 Here the subject is CORA's own configuration, which no substrate 

186 knows exists: an EPICS IOC cannot raise this error. In 

187 `ControlWriteRejectedError` the subject is the write, and the 

188 verdict arrives FROM the substrate, which is why that one carries a 

189 substrate-supplied `reason` and this one does not. No substrate 

190 spoke. 

191 

192 `scope` names which declaration refused, so an operator knows 

193 whether to change an env var or a route entry; `prefix` names the 

194 route when one carried the declaration. 

195 """ 

196 

197 def __init__( 

198 self, 

199 address: str, 

200 *, 

201 scope: Literal["deployment", "route"], 

202 prefix: str | None = None, 

203 ) -> None: 

204 detail = ( 

205 "control writes are disabled deployment-wide" 

206 if scope == "deployment" 

207 else f"route {prefix!r} is declared read-only" 

208 ) 

209 super().__init__(f"Control address {address!r} write refused: {detail}") 

210 self.address = address 

211 self.scope = scope 

212 self.prefix = prefix

215class ControlValueCoercionError(Exception): 

216 """A control value cannot be coerced to the kind a consumer requires. 

217 

218 `target_kind` names the kind the value could not satisfy; it is a 

219 free-form label, not constrained to `MeasurementKind`. Three live cases, 

220 all carrying the raw type label so the failure is debuggable rather 

221 than a silently dropped value: 

222 

223 - Adapter read-unpack: an adapter sees a structured type or a 

224 substrate primitive that does not fit the closed `MeasurementKind` 

225 set; `target_kind` is the `MeasurementKind` it was unpacking toward. 

226 - Adapter write-coercion: a write value cannot be encoded for the 

227 substrate; `target_kind` is an operation label (e.g. the PVA 

228 adapter's `"pva put"`), not a `MeasurementKind`. 

229 - Consumer-side: a `Measurement` unpacked cleanly but its `value` is 

230 the wrong type for a downstream consumer (e.g., an action body 

231 needs a numeric axis position but read a categorical leaf). Here 

232 `target_kind` is a LOGICAL kind the consumer names (e.g. 

233 `"number"`). Action bodies raise this so the Conductor records a 

234 structured step failure (it is a member of the Conductor's caught 

235 `_CONTROL_ERRORS`) instead of letting a bare `TypeError` escape. 

236 """ 

237 

238 def __init__(self, address: str, raw_type: str, target_kind: str) -> None: 

239 super().__init__( 

240 f"Control address {address!r} value of type {raw_type!r} " 

241 f"cannot coerce to kind {target_kind!r}" 

242 ) 

243 self.address = address 

244 self.raw_type = raw_type 

245 self.target_kind = target_kind

280@runtime_checkable 

281class ControlPort(Protocol): 

282 """Domain-shaped value-IO port for control-system addresses. 

283 

284 Substrate-agnostic. Concrete adapters 

285 (`InMemoryControlPort`, `CaprotoControlPort`, `EpicsCaControlPort`, 

286 `EpicsPvaControlPort`, future `TangoControlPort` / 

287 `OpcUaControlPort`) implement the wire details. Per 

288 [[project_non_determinism_principle]], port-injected effects are 

289 captured in the executor's event payloads at decider time. 

290 

291 Port shape follows the ros2_control "keep port dumb, executor 

292 pluggable" lesson from [[project_control_port_research]]. Adapter 

293 internals may use asyncio (aioca, p4p asyncio, asyncua) or 

294 threading (p4p thread, caproto sync backend); the surface callers 

295 see is `async def read` / `async def write` / `async def subscribe`. 

296 

297 The executor that consumes this Protocol stays open at v1: 

298 `ControlPort` composes with fixed-rate-tick, async event-driven, 

299 and plain-async executor architectures. The executor arc picks 

300 an initial shape; `ControlPort` is not coupled to that pick. 

301 

302 RPC (Tango Commands, OPC UA Methods) and typed events (Tango 

303 `DATA_READY` / `INTERFACE_CHANGE`, OPC UA `EventNotificationList`) 

304 are intentionally OUT of scope. Future sibling ports 

305 (`CommandPort`, `EventPort`) land at first concrete consumer 

306 trigger per adapter-first discipline. 

307 """

338 address unwritable, so no substrate was contacted).

342 def subscribe(self, address: str) -> AsyncIterator[Measurement]: 

343 """Subscribe to value changes on `address`. Caller iterates the result. 

344 

345 Plain `def`, no surrounding coroutine: caller pattern is 

346 `async for measurement in port.subscribe(address):`. Adapters may 

347 defer connect-and-register-as-subscriber to the first 

348 `__anext__`; a `ControlNotConnectedError` raised through the 

349 iterator (rather than from `subscribe()` itself) is the 

350 expected shape when the address never reaches a connected 

351 state. 

352 

353 On mid-stream disconnect the iterator raises 

354 `ControlNotConnectedError` so silent stream pause is 

355 impossible. The caller decides whether to re-subscribe or 

356 close. 

357 

358 Subscriber-responsible back-pressure. Adapters MAY coalesce 

359 intermediate values on lag (p4p Qt keep-last; OPC UA 

360 MonitoredItem queue policy); each adapter documents its 

361 coalescing policy in implementation notes. Cancellation is 

362 via the iterator's `aclose()`. 

363 """

367class SubstrateControlPort(Protocol[_AddressT_contra]): 

368 """Typed-address value-IO port implemented by concrete substrate adapters. 

369 

370 The registry-facing sibling of `ControlPort`. Where `ControlPort` is the 

371 caller-facing `str` surface, `SubstrateControlPort` is what 

372 `EpicsCaControlPort`, `EpicsPvaControlPort`, `CaprotoControlPort`, and 

373 `TangoControlPort` implement: it takes a `ControlAddress` (the typed sum 

374 parsed by `ControlPortRegistry` from the matched route's substrate). The 

375 adapter never re-parses a raw string; it reads the typed variant's fields 

376 directly (`EpicsPvAddress.pv`, `TangoAttributeAddress.device` / 

377 `.attribute`). 

378 

379 Generic in the address variant (contravariant): the EPICS adapters are 

380 `SubstrateControlPort[EpicsPvAddress]`, the Tango adapter is 

381 `SubstrateControlPort[TangoAttributeAddress]`. Each adapter legitimately 

382 accepts only its own variant. `ControlPortRegistry` holds routes as 

383 `SubstrateControlPort[Any]` because the substrate->variant correlation it 

384 guarantees (it parses the route's substrate into the matching variant) is 

385 a runtime invariant the type system cannot track statically. 

386 

387 `ControlPortRegistry` is the seam between the two surfaces: it implements 

388 `ControlPort` (`str`), and for each call parses the address into a 

389 `ControlAddress` and dispatches to the matched `SubstrateControlPort`. 

390 The exception families, `Measurement` shape, and subscribe semantics are 

391 identical to `ControlPort`; only the address type differs. 

392 """
