1"""ReachTier: how CORA reached a substrate for one observation. 

2 

3Originated in the Enclosure BC's permit-probe trail 

4([[project_enclosure_permit_probe_design]]) and hoisted here once a 

5second BC (Run, for the capture-observe seam) needed the same 

6vocabulary. `cora.run` cannot depend on `cora.enclosure.aggregates` 

7(tach), and the concept is substrate-neutral: it grades CORA's own 

8reach to whatever it is watching, never the thing being watched. 

9`cora.shared` has no dependencies of its own, so any BC may use this 

10without adding a cross-BC edge. 

11""" 

12 

13from enum import StrEnum 

14 

15 

16class ReachTier(StrEnum): 

17 """How CORA reached a substrate for one observation. 

18 

19 Two values ship. `RELAYED` means CORA received or fetched a value 

20 through the configured channel; `UNREACHED` means it could not, 

21 this tick. A stronger tier for a confirmed direct round trip to 

22 the authoritative source (as opposed to an intermediary, such as 

23 an EPICS CA gateway that may answer from its own cache) is 

24 deliberately NOT defined here: no producer in this codebase can 

25 currently prove one, and an unearned strong claim is worse than 

26 none. Adding a value later needs no migration in a consumer whose 

27 column is a length-CHECK rather than a value-enumerating CHECK. 

28 """ 

29 

30 RELAYED = "Relayed" 

31 UNREACHED = "Unreached" 

32 

33 

34__all__ = ["ReachTier"]
