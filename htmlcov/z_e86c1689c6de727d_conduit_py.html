1"""ConduitSummaryProjection: folds the ConduitDefined event into the 

2`proj_trust_conduit_summary` read model that backs `GET /conduits`. 

3 

4Subscribed events: 

5 - ConduitDefined -> INSERT (id + name + source_zone_id + 

6 target_zone_id + occurred_at) 

7 

8ConduitLogbookOpened/Closed events are intentionally NOT subscribed: 

9they are internal logbook bookkeeping (the entries store carries 

10verdict rows; the aggregate-level logbook events are for the 

11audit trail, not the summary projection). Same precedent as the 

12Decision summary projection skipping its DecisionLogbookOpened/Closed 

13events. A future `proj_trust_conduit_logbooks` join projection 

14covers "list conduits with N+ verdicts in window" if that use 

15case crystallizes. 

16 

17Conduit is immutable-once-defined today (lifecycle additions 

18deferred per the additive-state pattern in conduit/state.py). 

19"""

29_INSERT_CONDUIT_SQL = """ 

30INSERT INTO proj_trust_conduit_summary 

31 (conduit_id, name, source_zone_id, target_zone_id, created_at) 

32VALUES ($1, $2, $3, $4, $5) 

33ON CONFLICT (conduit_id) DO NOTHING 

34"""
