1"""The `DefineAssembly` command - intent dataclass for the define_assembly slice. 

2 

3Carries the caller-controlled structural fields: name, 

4presents_as (the global Role contracts the Assembly advertises), 

5required_slots, required_wires, the optional parameter_overrides_schema, 

6the optional drawing, and the optional operator-curatorial version label. 

7 

8Server-side concerns (new assembly_id, wall-clock timestamp, 

9correlation id, per-event ids, computed content_hash) are injected 

10by the handler from infrastructure ports or computed pre-emit. 

11 

12Slots and wires arrive as fully-constructed domain VOs (the route 

13layer's TemplateSlotBody / TemplateWireBody call .to_domain() before 

14the command is built); structural VO-level invariants (slot-name 

15length, cardinality enum membership, non-empty required_family_ids, 

16wire-port-name length, degenerate-full-self-loop rejection, wire- 

17endpoints-reference-declared-slots closure via Assembly.__post_init__

19or evolver-fold time, NOT inside the decider. 

20 

21Cross-aggregate references checked by the handler before the decider: 

22 - Every RoleId in presents_as must resolve to a defined Role. 

23 - Every FamilyId in every slot's required_family_ids must resolve. 

24 

25Cross-aggregate references NOT checked:
