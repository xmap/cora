1"""Decision bounded context. 

2 

3Owns the structured-audit story for every consequential choice in 

4CORA, human approval, AI inference, agent action, operator 

5override. Single aggregate (Decision), unified across deciders. 

6 

7## Why "decisions" is its own BC 

8 

9The Run BC introduced 4 free-form `reason` fields (RunAborted.reason, 

10RunStopped.reason, RunTruncated.reason, DiscardDataset.reason) and 

11the BC-map prose named that threshold as the "first non-trivial 

12decision needing structured audit." Free text is enough for the 

13moment of the action; structured audit is what the next regulator / 

14AI-act audit / scientific-replicability review will demand. 

15 

16## Aggregate is atomic-immutable; chains carry corrections 

17 

18Decisions are append-only. Corrections, exceptions, appeals, and 

19supersessions land as NEW Decisions with `parent_id` pointing at 

20the prior and `override_kind` explaining the transition. 

21 

22**Latest-in-chain wins** is the canonical projection rule for 

23consumers walking a Decision's parent chain. The aggregate-read 

24returns one Decision (the one identified by id); chain navigation 

25is the consumer's job. When a projection materializes "current 

26state of decision X," it walks parent_id back-pointers in the 

27inverse direction (x.parent → child(parent==x)) to find the latest 

28override. 

29 

30## Standards alignment (gate-review locks; 2026 survey + validation pass) 

31 

32 - **PROV-AGENT (eScience 2025)**: field naming aligns with 

33 PROV-O agent / activity vocabulary at the boundary; in-domain 

34 stays on primitives. 

35 - **NIST AI RMF + ISO/IEC 42001 + EU AI Act Article 12**: 

36 INSERT-only Postgres + event sourcing satisfies the automatic 

37 immutable record-keeping mandate by construction. 

38 - **ISO 17025 Clause 7.1.3 + ILAC-G8:09/2019**: `rule` 

39 + `inputs` carry the rule-and-its-inputs that a lab 

40 accreditation auditor will demand for any conformance decision. 

41 - **OPA Decision Logs**: PolicyGrant context's payload is 

42 isomorphic to OPA's `{decision_id, input, result, timestamp, 

43 metrics}` shape. `alternatives` carries the determining policy 

44 IDs Cedar-style. 

45 - **Anthropic extended-thinking signature**: `reasoning_signature` 

46 field provides per-decision content-addressing for tamper- 

47 evidence beyond row-level INSERT-only. 

48 - **OpenTelemetry GenAI semconv (gen_ai.*)**: AI-decider Logbook 

49 entries (8c) emit `gen_ai.request.model`, `gen_ai.usage.*`, 

50 `gen_ai.tool.*` etc. Aligning at carrier-time costs nothing, 

51 retrofitting later is painful. 

52 

53 

54Single aggregate (Decision). Three slices planned: 

55 - `register_decision` (8a, create-style; idempotency-wrapped; 

56 cross-aggregate validation via DecisionRegistrationContext for 

57 Actor + optional parent Decision) 

58 - `get_decision` (8a, read side; fold-on-read) 

59 - confidence_band derived field + rule registry doc + 

60 Cedar-style PolicyGrant convention (8b, light slice) 

61 - AI-decider Logbook integration with OpenTelemetry `gen_ai.*` 

62 attributes (8c) 

63 

64## Deferred-with-trigger 

65 

66 - Hard Run-BC reason migration (trigger: compliance audit 

67 demand or first structured-query consumer); planned path is 

68 additive `decision_id: UUID | None` on each terminal event, 

69 NOT replacement of `reason: str`. 

70 - OPA decision-log export endpoint (trigger: first OPA integration). 

71 - W3C VC 2.0 cross-org Decision export (trigger: first cross-org 

72 export request). 

73 - prEN 18229-1 / ISO/IEC DIS 24970 conformance (trigger: standard 

74 finalizes or first EU-AI-Act-Annex-III customer). 

75 

76Layout (mirrors every other BC): 

77 aggregates/decision/ -- aggregate state, events, evolver, read 

78 features/<verb>_decision/ -- vertical slice 

79 wire.py -- DecisionHandlers bundle + wire_decision(deps) 

80 routes.py -- register_decision_routes(app) 

81 tools.py -- register_decision_tools(mcp, *, get_handlers) 

82"""
