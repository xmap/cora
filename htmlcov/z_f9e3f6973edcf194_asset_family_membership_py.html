1"""AssetFamilyMembershipProjection: folds Asset<->Family membership 

2events into the `proj_equipment_asset_family_membership` read model. 

3 

4Per-row noun is `membership`: each row records that a specific Asset 

5is a member of a specific Family classification. The set-theoretic 

6framing reads naturally in both directions: "Families this Asset 

7belongs to" (per-Asset by primary key) and "Assets belonging to this 

8Family" (per-Family via the secondary index that the next phase's 

9"list Assets affording requirement X" diagnostic will use). 

10 

11Subscribed events: 

12 - AssetFamilyAdded -> INSERT (asset_id, family_id, added_at) 

13 with ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING for replay safety 

14 - AssetFamilyRemoved -> DELETE matching (asset_id, family_id) 

15 

16The aggregate state (`Asset.family_ids`) is the canonical source; this 

17projection mirrors the relation for cross-aggregate query 

18convenience. Aggregate `Asset.family_ids` answers "Families of this 

19Asset"; this projection answers both that and the reverse. 

20 

21Both event types are idempotent at the projection layer: 

22 - INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING tolerates duplicate Adds. 

23 - DELETE is naturally idempotent (DELETE of a non-existent row is 

24 a no-op). 

25The aggregate's decider already enforces strict-not-idempotent 

26semantics at command time (`AssetCannotAddFamilyError` on duplicate, 

27`AssetCannotRemoveFamilyError` on missing); the projection's 

28relaxed posture is the standard CORA pattern for replay safety. 

29 

30Forward-compat: the next phase extends `inspect_plan_binding`'s 

31diagnostic with "other Assets affording missing requirement X." That 

32extension MUST source its candidate set from THIS projection (a 

33single SQL join against `proj_equipment_family_summary` for the 

34affordance filter), NOT per-Asset event-stream replay. The reverse 

35index `_by_family_idx` exists for exactly that query pattern. 

36"""
