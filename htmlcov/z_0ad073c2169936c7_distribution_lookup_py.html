1"""DistributionLookup port: same-BC query for canonical Distribution by Dataset. 

2 

3Used by `seal_edition` to resolve the authoritative `DistributionUri` 

4/ `DatasetChecksum` / `byte_size` / `DatasetEncoding` for each member 

5Dataset before invoking the `EditionSerializer`. 

6 

7## Canonical pick policy 

8 

9`lookup_canonical_by_dataset(dataset_id)` returns the lowest-id 

10non-Discarded `Distribution` row for the given Dataset. The pick is 

11deterministic across re-queries; matches the canonical-pick rule in 

12the Edition design memo L8. 

13 

14## Convention 

15 

16Same-BC port (Data BC owns both Edition and Distribution); the 

17production adapter (`PostgresDistributionLookup`) queries 

18`proj_data_distribution_summary` by `dataset_id ORDER BY 

19distribution_id LIMIT 1 WHERE status != 'Discarded'`. Tests use 

20`InMemoryDistributionLookup` seeded directly. 

21 

22Lives in `cora.data.ports` rather than `cora.infrastructure.ports` 

23because it queries a Data-owned projection table; the port surface 

24is BC-internal even though its implementation reaches Postgres. 

25"""
