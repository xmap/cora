1"""The `ObserveSupplyStatus` command -- monitor-driven status observation. 

2 

3Per [[project_supply_monitor_trigger_design]]: carries the four 

4adapter-supplied fields needed to record a sensor-driven Supply 

5transition. 

6 

7 - `supply_id`: target Supply aggregate. 

8 - `new_status`: the status the adapter observed. The decider 

9 enforces FSM source-state allowlists per new_status AND fences 

10 Monitor out of `Recovering -> Available` + `Unknown -> Available` 

11 (operator-only per [[project_supply_design]] Anti-hooks). 

12 - `monitor_ref`: identifies the originating sensor / file / log 

13 (carried verbatim onto the emitted event for audit). 

14 - `monitor_source_id`: the stable `MonitorSourceId` UUID of the 

15 in-process adapter subscription that emitted the observation; 

16 threaded into the event payload's `triggered_by` field per 

17 [[project_fold_symmetry_design]]. Distinct from `monitor_ref`: 

18 `monitor_ref` is the human-readable "sensor identity" (PV name, 

19 file path) while `monitor_source_id` is the stable adapter 

20 subscription handle that survives sensor reconfigurations. 

21 - `reason`: free-text audit string per the existing Supply 

22 transition convention (1-500 chars after trim). 

23 

24`observed_at` is NOT on the command: the handler injects it from 

25the Clock port at call time (cross-BC non-determinism principle). 

26The adapter's wall-clock at observation is captured on the 

27SUBSCRIPTION side, not threaded through the command surface. 

28""" 

29
