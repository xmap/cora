1"""Facility aggregate state, enums, value objects, and domain errors. 

2 

3A `Facility` is a per-code singleton record naming a containment / 

4authority entity that other CORA aggregates bind to (Asset.facility_id 

5in slice 8, Supply.facility_id in slice 7, Seal/Permit/Credential 

6facility_id binding in slice 6, ClearanceTemplate.facility_id in slice 

79). Per [[project_structural_scope_design]] the aggregate resolves the 

8upper Asset tiers / SupplyScope.Facility / Federation.facility_id 

9masquerade family in one promotion. 

10 

11Two-tier identity per locked design: 

12 

13 - `Facility.id: FacilityId` is the internal-opaque PK for spine 

14 references within ONE deployment. 

15 - `Facility.code: FacilityCode` is the cross-deployment convergent 

16 identity. Cross-BC and cross-deployment references MUST use 

17 `Facility.code`; the architecture fitness test 

18 `test_cross_bc_refs_facility_code_not_id` enforces this. 

19 

20Day-one `FacilityKind` is closed at `{Site, Area}`: 

21 

22 - `Site` (ISA-95 Site): the physical research facility (APS, MAX IV). 

23 Only kind that holds `trust_anchor_credential_ids` and that may 

24 have `parent_id = None`. 

25 - `Area` (ISA-95 Area): an experimental hall or building within a 

26 Site. Always has a non-null parent; trust anchors stay empty 

27 (Area inherits the parent Site's trust posture). 

28 

29`Institution` (ISA-95 Enterprise / PROV-O Organization / ROR) is 

30DEFERRED; when promoted it becomes a separate `Organization` aggregate, 

31NOT a `FacilityKind` member, to avoid the ISA-95 Enterprise/Site 

32conflation. 

33 

34`Sector` is DEFERRED; APS-local concept whose canonical home is an 

35Asset row at the Area tier (per [[project_supply_sector_disposition]]). 

36Map APS Sector to `kind=Area` with a documented operational convention 

37until MAX IV / DLS bring a second intermediate vocabulary that doesn't 

38fit Area. 

39 

40Two-state FSM per locked design: 

41 

42 - `Active` (genesis from `register_facility`). 

43 - `Decommissioned` (terminal, via `decommission_facility`; no further 

44 transitions). 

45 

46Fold-symmetric attribution pairs per [[project_fold_symmetry_design]]: 

47`registered_at + registered_by` at genesis; `decommissioned_at + 

48decommissioned_by` at terminal transition. Both `_by` fields are 

49typed `ActorId` so the fold-symmetry fitness test detects them 

50structurally. 

51 

52PIDINST day-one carriage per locked design: 

53 

54 - `alternate_identifiers: frozenset[AlternateIdentifier]` is accepted 

55 as an optional seed in `register_facility` (defaults to empty). 

56 Mirrors the Asset register-time seed; add/remove slices are 

57 deferred per Asset precedent. 

58 - `persistent_id: PersistentIdentifier | None` ships on state only 

59 (default None). The `assign_facility_persistent_id` slice is 

60 deferred (mirrors Asset's separate `assign_asset_persistent_id` 

61 slice; trigger is first Facility-tier DOI mint). 

62 

63Trust-anchor binding per locked design: 

64 

65 - `trust_anchor_credential_ids: frozenset[CredentialId]` carries the 

66 set of Credential ids the Facility trusts as Seal anchors; 

67 populated only when `kind=Site`. The Seal decider checks set- 

68 membership against this field (initialize_seal + 

69 rotate_seal_online_key) to enforce the structural cross-tenant 

70 defense, raising `SealCredentialNotTrustAnchorError` on miss. 

71 Mutated via `add_facility_trust_anchor_credential` and 

72 `remove_facility_trust_anchor_credential`. 

73"""

369@dataclass(frozen=True) 

370class Facility: 

371 """Aggregate root: a per-code Facility singleton. 

372 

373 Two-tier identity: `id` is the opaque UUID PK for spine references 

374 within one deployment; `code` is the cross-deployment convergent 

375 slug. Cross-BC and cross-deployment references MUST use `code` 

376 (architecture fitness test enforces this in slice 5 Sub-Slice D). 

377 

378 Structural invariants enforced in `__post_init__` and re-enforced 

379 at the `register_facility` decider (belt-and-braces): 

380 

381 - Site has no parent: `kind == Site` implies 

382 `parent_id is None` (else `FacilitySiteCannotHaveParentError`). 

383 - Area has parent: `kind == Area` implies 

384 `parent_id is not None` (else `FacilityAreaMustHaveParentError`). 

385 - Area has empty trust anchors: `kind == Area` implies 

386 `trust_anchor_credential_ids == frozenset()` (else 

387 `FacilityAreaCannotHaveTrustAnchorsError`). 

388 

389 Per [[project_fold_symmetry_design]] the genesis envelope 

390 `occurred_at` is folded onto state as `registered_at` alongside the 

391 identity denorm `registered_by`. The terminal transition mirrors 

392 the pair: `decommissioned_at` and `decommissioned_by` are 

393 populated together by the `FacilityDecommissioned` evolver fold. 

394 Both `_by` fields are typed `ActorId` so the fold-symmetry fitness 

395 test detects attribution fields structurally. 

396 """ 

397 

398 id: FacilityId 

399 code: FacilityCode 

400 display_name: FacilityName 

401 kind: FacilityKind 

402 parent_id: FacilityId | None 

403 trust_anchor_credential_ids: frozenset[CredentialId] 

404 status: FacilityStatus 

405 persistent_id: PersistentIdentifier | None 

406 alternate_identifiers: frozenset[AlternateIdentifier] 

407 registered_at: datetime 

408 registered_by: ActorId 

409 decommissioned_at: datetime | None 

410 decommissioned_by: ActorId | None 

411 

412 def __post_init__(self) -> None: 

413 if self.kind is FacilityKind.SITE and self.parent_id is not None: 

414 raise FacilitySiteCannotHaveParentError(self.code, self.parent_id) 

415 if self.kind is FacilityKind.AREA and self.parent_id is None: 

416 raise FacilityAreaMustHaveParentError(self.code) 

417 if self.kind is FacilityKind.AREA and self.trust_anchor_credential_ids: 

418 raise FacilityAreaCannotHaveTrustAnchorsError(self.code)
