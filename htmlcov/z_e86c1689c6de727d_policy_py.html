1"""PolicySummaryProjection: folds the PolicyDefined event into the 

2`proj_trust_policy_summary` read model that backs `GET /policies`. 

3 

4Subscribed events: 

5 - PolicyDefined -> INSERT (id + name + conduit_id + occurred_at) 

6 

7The list-typed `permitted_principal_ids` and `permitted_commands` 

8payload fields are intentionally NOT projected: they are list-shaped 

9and a future `proj_trust_policy_principals` join projection covers 

10"list policies allowing Principal X" if that use case crystallizes 

11(analog to the deferred `proj_recipe_method_capabilities` join 

12documented in recipe/projections/method.py). 

13 

14Policy is immutable-once-defined today (the 

15Drafted -> Approved -> Active -> Superseded lifecycle per BC-map 

16is deferred per the additive-state pattern in policy/state.py). 

17"""

27_INSERT_POLICY_SQL = """ 

28INSERT INTO proj_trust_policy_summary 

29 (policy_id, name, conduit_id, created_at)

31ON CONFLICT (policy_id) DO NOTHING 

32"""
