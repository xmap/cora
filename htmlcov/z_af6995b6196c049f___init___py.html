1"""Visit aggregate: state, errors, events, evolver, read repo. 

2 

3Vertical slices that operate on this aggregate live under 

4`cora.trust.features.<verb>_visit/` and import from here for state and 

5event types. 

6 

7Per `[[project_visit_aggregate_design]]`, Visit is the operational- 

8envelope counterpart to Trust's authz-topology primitives. 8-state FSM 

9locked day one: Planned -> Arrived -> InProgress <-> OnHold -> 

10Completed; +Cancelled (pre-work) +Aborted (mid-work) +Voided 

11(registration-error). Concerns are layered as additive slices: lifecycle 

12(register + 8 transitions), presence (check-in / check-out), and 

13Surface control (take / release). 

14"""
