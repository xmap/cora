1"""Pure decider for the `RecordWitnessedRun` command: the witnessed genesis. 

2 

3Second genesis decider on the Run aggregate, after `start_run`. Where 

4`start_run` drives the act through CORA's own Conductor and hardcodes 

5`ConductMode.CONDUCTED`, this decider records that an external tool 

6already began a capture and hardcodes `ConductMode.WITNESSED`. Never the 

7other way around: `RecordWitnessedRun` carries no `conduct_mode` field for 

8either decider to read, so the mode is a property of which decider ran. 

9 

10## The governing rule: refuse on what CORA can fix, witness what CORA cannot 

11 

12CORA-side data faults (deprecated Plan, decommissioned Asset, capability 

13shortfall, bad wires, an absent Clearance or Supply) stay refusals here 

14exactly as at a driven start: they are loud, fixable, and the watcher 

15retries on the next capture. Only the two genuinely external live 

16facility signals, enclosure permit and beam availability, are witnessed 

17instead of enforced: `witness_safety_envelope` (shared with 

18`check_safety_envelope` via the same four gate functions in 

19`safety_envelope.py`, so the two paths cannot silently drift) still 

20raises for a failing clearance or supply gate, and returns a 

21`SafetyEnvelopeVerdict` for the other two. 

22 

23## Validation order 

24 

25Mirrors `start_run.decide`'s order for every CORA-side check it shares; 

26the trigger guard runs first (it is a request-shape rejection, not a 

27domain-state one) and the envelope witness runs where `start_run` runs 

28its enforcing envelope check. 

29 

30Invariants: 

31 - State must be None (genesis-only) -> RunAlreadyExistsError 

32 - `trigger` must be the literal "Monitor" -> RunMonitorTriggerNotPermittedError 

33 - At least one Clearance must reference this Run's scope 

34 -> RunRequiresActiveClearanceError 

35 - At least one referencing Clearance must be Active 

36 -> RunClearanceCoverageMismatchError 

37 - Every kind in Method.needed_supplies must have at least 

38 one registered Supply -> RunRequiresAvailableSupplyError 

39 - Every kind in Method.needed_supplies must have at least 

40 one AVAILABLE Supply -> RunSupplyCoverageMismatchError 

41 - Plan must not be Deprecated -> RunBoundPlanDeprecatedError 

42 - Subject (when set) must be Mounted or Measured 

43 -> RunSubjectNotMountableError 

44 - No bound Asset may be Decommissioned 

45 -> RunPlanAssetDecommissionedError 

46 - Union of current bound Asset families must cover Method's 

47 needed_family_ids -> RunCapabilitiesNotSatisfiedError 

48 - Effective parameters (the Plan's own defaults, unmodified: this 

49 command carries no override_parameters) must validate against 

50 Method's parameters_schema -> InvalidRunEffectiveParametersError 

51 (via validate_effective_parameters_against_method_schema) 

52 - All Plan wires must re-validate against current Asset.ports 

53 -> PlanWireAssetNotBoundError / PlanWirePortNotFoundError 

54 (via validate_wire_endpoints) 

55 - Name must be valid -> InvalidRunNameError (via RunName VO) 

56 

57Not enforced (witnessed instead, see above): the enclosure permit and 

58beam availability gates. Not present at all: campaign membership, 

59pinned_calibration_ids, input_dataset_ids, compute reachability -- the 

60command carries none of the fields those checks key on, so there is 

61nothing to validate. 

62"""
