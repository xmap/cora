1"""Projection-worker framework. 

2 

3Public concepts: 

4 

5 - `Projection` Protocol: the read-side fold a BC writes to maintain 

6 a `proj_<bc>_<name>` queryable table from the event stream. Per 

7 BC, lives in `cora.<bc>.projections.<name>`. Fast, batch large 

8 (`batch_size=100`), idempotent at the SQL layer. 

9 

10 - `Reaction` Protocol: side-effecting Subscriber that emits NEW 

11 events (often cross-BC) or calls the outside world (LLM, signer, 

12 storage). Per BC, lives in `cora.<bc>.subscribers.<name>`. Slow, 

13 batch small (`batch_size=1`), idempotent via deterministic 

14 UUIDv5 stream id + ConcurrencyError-as-no-op. Recovery from a 

15 wedged bookmark is the `dismiss_event_in_reaction` operator slice. 

16 

17 - `ProjectionRegistry`: the worker iterates this. Each BC registers 

18 its projections via `register_<bc>_projections(registry, deps)` 

19 and its reactions via `register_<bc>_subscribers(registry, deps)`, 

20 both called by the composition root during lifespan setup. The 

21 class name kept for backward compatibility; the registry accepts 

22 any Subscriber (Projection or Reaction). 

23 

24 - `projection_worker_lifespan(deps, registry, settings)`: async 

25 context manager the FastAPI lifespan wraps. Spawns the worker 

26 when the registry is non-empty + a Postgres pool is available; 

27 no-ops in the in-memory test environment. 

28 

29 - `drain_projections(pool, registry, deadline_seconds)`: integration- 

30 test helper that synchronously advances every registered subscriber 

31 until each bookmark catches up to the head position (or raises 

32 `ProjectionDrainTimeoutError`). Avoids `asyncio.sleep` flakiness. 

33 

34 - `encode_cursor` / `decode_cursor`: opaque base64 round-trip for 

35 `(created_at, UUID)` keyset-pagination cursors. Locked convention 

36 per Phase-8e D9: every `proj_*` table includes both columns; every 

37 list endpoint uses these helpers so cursor format is uniform across 

38 BCs. 

39 

40Internal primitive: `Subscriber` Protocol that both Projection and 

41Reaction satisfy structurally. Not exported publicly because BC 

42authors should write Projections or Reactions, not bare Subscribers. 

43"""
